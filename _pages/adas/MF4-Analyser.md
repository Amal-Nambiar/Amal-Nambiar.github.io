    <h2>Overview</h2>
    <p>MF4 Analyser provides utilities to read, visualize and extract signals from MF4 files used in vehicle telemetry and testing.</p>

    <h3>Capabilities</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Signal extraction and plotting</li>
      <li>Export to CSV and light processing</li>
      <li>Batch analysis for multiple runs</li>
    </ul>
