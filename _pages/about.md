I'm Amal Nambiar, a Lead Engineer at Mahindra & Mahindra working in ADAS validation and algorithm development for driver-assistance systems, including Autonomous Emergency Braking, Lane Keeping Assist, Emergency Lane Keeping, and Adaptive Cruise Control.

My work spans both system validation and algorithm development: building and validating MIL/SIL/VIL workflows in MATLAB/Simulink and SCANeR Studio, while also tuning perception and control logic, engineering state estimation pipelines, and implementing motion-planning approaches such as A*, Dijkstra, and RRT variants.

Before this role, I completed my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras, where my thesis focused on nonlinear MEMS energy harvesting under vortex-induced vibration using magnetic interaction. That research deepened my interest in nonlinear dynamics, multiscale modeling, and building physically grounded engineering solutions for difficult real-world problems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of theory and implementation — translating physics and estimation principles into robust simulation-driven engineering decisions that ultimately improve real systems.
